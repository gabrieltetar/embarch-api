## Choosing a mode

Not written yet. The "which mode" table is in the `embarch-hil` skill; the design is in `embarch-hil-proposal.md`.

The three modes are `live`, `study` and `test`. Run `embarch-api guide hil <mode>` for one.

## Live

Not written yet. Live is exploration during development: an answer now, a live log, findings written back. The design is in `embarch-hil-proposal.md`.

## Study

Not written yet. A study is a custom question answered by measurement on exactly one bench, ending in a report. The design is in `embarch-hil-proposal.md`.

## Test

Not written yet. A test is technical conformance with a verdict per test, on one or more benches. The design is in `embarch-hil-proposal.md`.
